# Research

**Status: not implemented.**

**Owning stage: Stage 06 onward.**

## Intended responsibility

Hold hypothesis registration, the append-only experiment ledger, baselines and negative controls, walk-forward design, anti-overfitting analysis, and strategy reports.

Every candidate begins as a registered hypothesis with an economic rationale, exact observable inputs, a forecast horizon, a baseline, falsification criteria, cost assumptions, and a maximum search budget declared in advance.

## Premature implementation is forbidden

No strategy research may begin before the data, accounting, execution, risk, and validation foundations exist. That ordering is the method, not a formality: a promising result obtained before the falsification harness exists cannot be distinguished from a lucky one.

Baselines come before complex models. Statistical correction comes before selecting a winner. The final holdout is inspected once, after the design is frozen; if it changes the design, it is no longer a holdout.

An unadjusted p-value obtained after searching many configurations may never be reported as though it came from a single predeclared test.

Nothing in this area may be built before its stage begins, and its stage may not begin before the stages it depends on have been reviewed and accepted. Building ahead of the gate produces work that was never validated against the evidence the earlier stages were meant to produce.
